Model fit: GWR vs MGWR (Lama & Sun Table 5)

MGWR bandwidth and significance by variable

Bandwidth is the number of nearest SA1s each local regression uses: small means the relationship varies locally, large means it's close to global. "Significant" uses MGWR's multiple-testing-corrected t-values. VIF (variance inflation factor) above 10, in red, means the variable is almost a linear combination of the others. Its coefficient is then unstable and shouldn't be read on its own. The paper uses counts (population, employed, educated, income earners), which all grow with SA1 size.
