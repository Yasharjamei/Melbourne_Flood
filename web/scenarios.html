How accurate is the placement?real result

Error is WAPE: the total absolute error divided by total residents. "Within" is the share of units within 10 people or 20% of the Census count. SA2 totals were set to the true 2021 values, so this measures placement only; real forecasts add the error of the totals themselves. On this evidence the page shows suburbs and SA2s as headline figures, SA1s only with ranges, and never single mesh blocks.

Method

  • Totals: Victoria in Future 2023 projections, checked against ABS Estimated Resident Population by SA2 up to 2025.
  • Placement: each SA2's growth goes to the mesh blocks where Vicmap addresses were created at least 12 months earlier, at a fitted residents-per-new-address rate; existing homes keep their 2021 count. Planned redevelopment and greenfield sites carry the trend forward to 2036.
  • Cross-check: GHSL built-up and population grids (1975–2030, 100 m, from satellite imagery) flag SA2s where the address-based growth and the satellite trend disagree.
  • Flood areas: today's planning flood overlays, as on the main map, plus the sea-level-rise inundation extents for each scenario.
  • Ranges: from the back-test error at each scale, widened by the spread between projection sources.
