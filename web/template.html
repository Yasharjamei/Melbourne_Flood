Circle figures are estimates. Each SA1's counts are split across its mesh blocks in proportion to where people live, and a circle counts the mesh blocks whose centre falls inside it. With a council selected, circles count residents of that council only. Age–sex shares within an SA1 are assumed the same across its mesh blocks. ABS perturbation means totals can differ slightly between tables.
