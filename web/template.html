Lama & Sun group everyone under 20 and over 59 as one "dependent population". The pyramid above shows why that hides the difference between a young family suburb and an ageing one: they need different evacuation plans.

Census 2021 General Community Profile (ABS) by SA1; SA1 and LGA boundaries, ASGS 2021. Flood extents are Victorian planning scheme overlays: Land Subject to Inundation and Floodway (riverine) and Special Building Overlay (overland flow), Vicmap Planning, DataVic.

Circle figures are estimates. Each SA1's counts are split across its mesh blocks in proportion to where people live, and a circle counts the mesh blocks whose centre falls inside it. Age–sex shares within an SA1 are assumed the same across its mesh blocks. ABS perturbation means totals can differ slightly between tables.
