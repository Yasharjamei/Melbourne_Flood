Who lives in Melbourne's flood areas

People living in flood-prone areas, neighbourhood by neighbourhood

How to read this

Each column is one neighbourhood (an ABS SA2, about the size of a suburb) in Greater Melbourne's 31 councils.

Height: how many residents live inside flood-prone areas: planning-scheme flood overlays and the coastal inundation extent.

Colour: change since 2025, from no change (cyan) to six times more (pink).

First the city grows to 2036. Then a 1-in-100-year storm tide arrives as the sea rises.
